Practice questions in the style of the Adventure and Beacon papers.

Skipton Girls' High School's Year 7 entry test (run by FSCE) uses two papers: Adventure (multiple choice, English and maths) and Beacon (short written answers, English and maths). This page has original practice questions written in that same format and style — they are not the real test questions, which stay confidential to FSCE. For the genuine examples, see the school's own familiarisation guide on their admissions page.
