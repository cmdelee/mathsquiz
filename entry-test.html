Entry Test Practice

Practice questions in the style of the Adventure and Beacon papers.

Several grammar and independent schools use an FSCE-run Year 7 entry test with two papers: Adventure (multiple choice, English and maths) and Beacon (short written answers, English and maths). This page has original practice questions written in that same format and style — they are not the real test questions, which stay confidential to FSCE. For genuine examples, check whichever school you're applying to for their own familiarisation guide.
